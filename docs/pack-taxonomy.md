## Inclusion Criteria

A tool lands in a pack when its primary use case matches the one-line criterion below. Multi-pack tools (e.g., a calculator useful both at home and in finance) appear in both lists.

- **travel** — A tool helps with travel logistics: clocks across regions, countdown to an event, or date difference calculations.
- **finance** — A tool produces a numeric money result: income, EMI, growth, tip, discount, or similar monetary calculation.
- **study** — A tool supports academic or learning workflows: grading, GPA, focus sessions, or text generation.
- **developer** — A tool manipulates structured text or developer-facing data: JSON, regex, encoding, URL, base64, or random.
- **household** — A tool helps with a household or personal-life task: health metrics, animal fun, decisions, color picking, or general life math.
