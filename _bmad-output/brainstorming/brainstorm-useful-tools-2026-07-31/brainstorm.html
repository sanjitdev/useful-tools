04 ·

Job to Be Done — the hire.

12 jobs phrased as users speaking in the first person. The label column carries the job ID; the body is the user's exact words.

JTBD-1Users hire us to answer a small, private question right now — no install, no signup, no tracking. Every UX choice reduces friction on that.
JTBD-2Users hire us to stop context-switching when a calculator question shows up in a meeting, email, or doc. Type-and-answer in under five seconds.
JTBD-3Users hire us to compute something they'll paste into Slack or email. Auto-copy the result; the next click goes to the chat.
JTBD-4Users hire us to share a calculation with a colleague or client. Sharable URLs recreate the exact inputs on load.
JTBD-5Users hire us to feel in control of small daily decisions. Decision wheel, pros/cons, Eisenhower matrix — emotional tools, not just functional.
JTBD-6Users hire us to satisfy a curiosity without social-media shame. Fun tools (animal race, space, lifespan) are the brand-building hooks.
JTBD-7Users hire us to teach my kid or team a concept visually. Embeddable mini-tools, classroom mode, big result, no chrome.
JTBD-8Users hire us to fall back to when my phone app is full of ads. Zero ads, no sign-in, no tracking — the value prop itself.
JTBD-9Users hire us to trust the math is right. Show formula, show units, show edge cases, never silently round. Show your work.
JTBD-10Users hire us to make a tiny decision without thinking about the tool. UX goal: muscle memory. ⌘K → 4 chars → Enter under 1.5s.
JTBD-11Users hire us to bookmark the one tool they love. Deep-linkable URLs, PWA install to home screen, recent + favorites.
JTBD-12Users hire us to look at a number and remember the why. History is a feature. Per-tool history plus a global history timeline.

08 ·

Quality — twelve gates, each one a turnstile.

Twelve gates every tool must pass to leave the bench. Each card names the gate and the rule behind it.

G1QUAL-1Tool Contract. Metadata, mount/destroy, accessible labels, live result, state persistence, URL share, print, sample data, error recovery, tests.
G2QUAL-2Measurable budgets. Home LCP <1.5s mid-mobile; tool interactive <1s; zero CLS; no render-blocking third parties.
G3QUAL-3Accessibility gate. Keyboard-only completion, visible focus, landmarks, WCAG AA, reduced-motion, screen-reader, 44px touch targets.
G4QUAL-4Correctness gate. Pure calculations isolated from DOM; golden vectors, boundaries, invalid inputs, units, explicit rounding.
G5QUAL-5Privacy gate. No analytics by default, no external requests, privacy dashboard, export/import, transparent keys, sensitive warnings.
G6QUAL-6Offline gate. Service worker caches shell + tool assets; offline page; no CDN fonts/icons; versioned cache migration; offline indicator.
G7QUAL-7Internationalization. Message catalogs; locale-aware number/date/currency; RTL-safe CSS; Bengali, Hindi, Spanish starter locales.
G8QUAL-8Browser support. Current Chrome/Firefox/Safari/Edge; graceful file://; no unsupported API without fallback; feature detection, no UA sniffing.
G9QUAL-9Visual consistency. Shared tokens, spacing, controls, result cards, alerts, headers, empty/error states, dark + high-contrast themes.
G10QUAL-10Release gate. Metadata entry; link, mobile, a11y, offline, print smoke tests; changelog note.
G11QUAL-11Observability without tracking. Local diagnostics panel — timings, errors, cache state, capabilities — copy-diagnostics for bug reports.
G12QUAL-12Error quality. Preserve input, identify the bad field, explain the fix, no alert(), retry/reset/sample, never silently coerce.

You arrive here.

Seven insights, hung on the back wall of the workbench. Each one earns its own glyph — the kind of mark you'd etch into a wooden beam.

INSIGHT · 01 ✦

The differentiation is not tool count — it is a local-first, privacy-forward, keyboard-first tool operating system with unusually high correctness and accessibility.

INSIGHT · 02 ⌘

Architecture must collapse duplication: shared Tool Contract, shared design tokens, shared header/footer, single home data source, one command palette, one settings modal.

INSIGHT · 03 ⌖

Core positioning — "The local-first tool kit for the rest of the internet." Not feature count, but trust, speed, keyboard-first, embeddable, copy-and-share-by-default.

INSIGHT · 04 ∞

The strongest expansion is workflow packs — Travel, Finance, Study, Developer, Household — built from composable tools. Depth and repeat use, not a random catalog.

INSIGHT · 05 ⚓

Capture moats — shared URL state, embeddable widgets, keyboard shortcuts, PWA install, plain-language intent routing, no-tracking badge, classroom mode.

INSIGHT · 06 ▰

Quality moats — measurable per-tool score (a11y / mobile / offline / speed / print / share / tests), gated to 8/10 minimum before a tool is published.

INSIGHT · 07 ❦

Brand moat — privacy dashboard, transparent storage, view-source link, per-tool contributor credits, tried X? also try Y related-tools trail.

"Add depth, not breadth. Stay on the bench until the wood remembers your hands."
